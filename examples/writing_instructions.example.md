# Writing Instructions

These are your personal instructions for the cover-letter writer. Be specific: include tone, emphasis, facts to mention, and things to avoid.

## Voice & Tone

- Keep it direct and conversational; no flowery openings or corporate jargon.
- Be confident but honest. Show genuine interest in the company, not just the job title.
- Avoid hedging ("I believe I might be able to..."). If you claim something, own it.

## What to Emphasize

- Mention that I can start within 4 weeks if relevant.
- Emphasize hands-on experience with backend systems and distributed systems thinking.
- Highlight projects where I took initiative or solved a real problem, not just "participated."
- German: Betone praktische Erfahrung über Zertifikate.

## Facts to Include (if Relevant)

- I have shipped production services and debugged them at 2am.
- I care about code quality and clean architecture, not just "getting it done."

## Things to Avoid

- Don't make it sound like I'm applying to every job in the city. Be specific about why *this* role.
- Don't mention salary unless the ad asks.
- Don't apologize or minimize my experience.

## Examples of Good Openings

- "I'm drawn to [company] because [specific reason: product, tech stack, problem they solve]."
- "Your job description mentions X; I've spent the last two years solving exactly that."

## Language Preference

- German and English are equally fine; match the ad's language.
